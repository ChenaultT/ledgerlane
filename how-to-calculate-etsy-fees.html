How to Calculate Etsy Fees in 2026: A Worked Example for US Sellers

If you've ever looked at an Etsy deposit and thought "that's less than I expected," you're not alone. Etsy charges several separate fees, they're calculated on different amounts, and some are taken per order while others are charged when you list or advertise. Once you know the formula, though, you can calculate the fees on any order in under a minute and check whether your monthly statement adds up.

Figures below are for sellers whose payment account is in the US, checked on Etsy's Help Center on October 14, 2026. Etsy can change its fees, so confirm on the linked pages before you rely on them.

The fees that apply to most US orders

Fee Rate Calculated on
Listing fee $0.20 per listing Charged when you list, and again on each auto-renewal (listings expire after 4 months) and for each additional quantity sold
Transaction fee 6.5% The order amount, including shipping and gift wrap you charge
Payment processing (US) 3% + $0.25 per order The total sale price, including shipping and any sales tax
Offsite Ads (only on attributed orders) 15%, or 12% if your shop has made $10,000+ in a 365-day period The order total; capped at $100 per order

Sources: Etsy fees and taxes, payment processing fees, Offsite Ads.

Other charges you may see: Etsy Ads (you set a daily budget and pay per click), Etsy shipping labels, a one-time shop set-up fee in some locations, a 2.5% currency conversion fee if your listing currency differs from your bank account currency, and optional subscriptions such as Etsy Plus.

The formula for one order

For a typical US order without Offsite Ads:

Etsy fees = $0.20 listing + 6.5% × (item price + shipping + gift wrap) + 3% × (total paid incl. tax) + $0.25

Note the one subtle difference: the payment processing fee is charged on the full amount the buyer paid, including sales tax, while the transaction fee is based on the order amount you set.

Example 1: a $24 digital download

A buyer purchases a $24 digital file. There's no shipping, and the buyer's state charges $1.68 in sales tax, which Etsy collects and remits.

  • Listing fee: $0.20
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% × $24.00 = $1.56
  • Payment processing: 3% × ($24.00 + $1.68) + $0.25 = $0.77 + $0.25 = $1.02
  • Total Etsy fees: $2.78, leaving $21.22 of the $24 sale (about 11.6% in fees)

The sales tax itself isn't your money. It's added to the buyer's total and passed through, but it does increase the processing fee slightly.

Example 2: a $30 physical item with $5 shipping

  • Order amount: $30 + $5 = $35. Sales tax collected: $2.45.
  • Listing fee: $0.20
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% × $35.00 = $2.28 (2.275, rounded)
  • Payment processing: 3% × $37.45 + $0.25 = $1.37
  • Total Etsy fees: about $3.85

Two things matter here. Shipping is part of the fee base, so the fee on "free shipping" built into the price is the same as on shipping charged separately. And the shipping label you buy is a separate cost on top of these fees.

Example 3: the same order through Offsite Ads

If the buyer clicked an Etsy ad on another site and bought within 30 days, the Offsite Ads fee is added. For a shop under the $10,000 threshold: 15% × $35 = $5.25. Total fees jump to about $9.10 on that order. For US sellers, Etsy doesn't apply the Offsite Ads fee to sales tax.

Shops that have never reached $10,000 in a 365-day period can opt out of Offsite Ads. Once a shop crosses that threshold, participation is required and the rate drops to 12%. Either way, it's worth pricing so that an Offsite Ads sale still makes money.

Don't forget the per-listing costs

The $0.20 listing fee isn't charged per order, which makes it easy to overlook:

  • Each new listing costs $0.20, whether it sells or not.
  • With auto-renew on, a listing that sells renews and is charged another $0.20.
  • Listings that don't sell expire after 4 months and are charged again if they auto-renew.
  • If a buyer purchases 3 of the same item, you pay $0.20 for each additional quantity.

For a small shop the amounts are modest, but they add up across hundreds of listings and renewals.

How to check your fees against your monthly statement

Calculating fees on one order is useful. Checking a whole month is what catches surprises. Here's a simple routine:

  1. Download two CSVs from Shop Manager: the Orders CSV (sold orders) and the Monthly Statement CSV from your Payment account.
  2. Total your order amounts for the month: item totals, shipping and sales tax.
  3. Calculate expected fees: 6.5% of item + shipping, 3% of the full amount including tax plus $0.25 per order, and $0.20 per listing, renewal or extra quantity.
  4. Total the actual fees on the statement by type (transaction, processing, listing).
  5. Compare. Small differences come from rounding, refunds (Etsy credits fees proportionally when you refund) and timing across month-end. Larger gaps are worth a closer look.
  6. List the other charges separately: Etsy Ads, Offsite Ads, shipping labels and subscriptions. These are usually the biggest swing factors in your real profit.

Fees and your taxes

Etsy fees are a cost of running your shop, so keep a clean yearly total by type. Your tax preparer will want them. Also keep in mind:

  • Online marketplaces must send a Form 1099-K when payments for goods or services exceed $20,000 in more than 200 transactions, and may send one for lower amounts (IRS).
  • With or without a 1099-K, you must report all of your business income.
  • Sales tax that Etsy collects and remits for the buyer generally isn't your income, but it can appear in your gross figures, which is another reason to reconcile.

This article is general information, not tax advice. Ask a qualified tax professional about your situation.

FAQ

What fees does Etsy charge US sellers in 2026?

A 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping and gift wrap, a payment processing fee of 3% + $0.25 on the total the buyer paid (including sales tax), and $0.20 per listing, renewal and extra quantity sold. Offsite Ads add 15% (12% for shops over $10,000 in a 365-day period, capped at $100) on sales that come from those ads.

Is the processing fee charged on sales tax?

Yes. Etsy's US payment processing fee is calculated on the full amount the buyer paid, including sales tax, while the transaction fee is based on the order amount you set (item price plus shipping).

How do I check that Etsy charged me the right fees?

Download your Monthly Statement CSV (Shop Manager, Finances, Monthly statements, Generate CSV), total each fee type, and compare it with the expected fees calculated from your Orders CSV. Small gaps usually come from rounding, refunds and month-end timing.
